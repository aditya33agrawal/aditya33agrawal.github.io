Investing philosophy

How I think about markets

Fundamentals first

Price is a vote. Earnings are the weighing machine. Every position is anchored on five-year financials before the chart is opened.

Sector context, always

A 15% ROE is brilliant in cement and average in private banks. Comparison is the analyst's first job.

Behaviour over prediction

Nobody knows what the market does next week. The edge is in not selling when conviction is clear.

Accessibility matters

Good analysis should not be reserved for large portfolios. Every methodology I build is fully documented so any reader can audit it.
